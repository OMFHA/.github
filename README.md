**The Ogden Masonic Fraternal Home Association (OMFHA), also referred to as the Masonic Fraternal Home Association of Ogden,** is a 501(c)(10) fraternal organization based in Ogden, Utah. It serves as a central hub and property-holding entity for several Masonic lodges and related fraternal bodies.

### Key Facts
- **EIN**: 87-0151605
- **Year Formed**: 1999
- **Address**: PO Box 1085, Ogden, UT 84402 (Weber County)
- **Primary Location**: It operates in connection with the **Ogden Masonic Temple** at 1240 20th Street (at Harrison Boulevard), Ogden, UT. This historic building houses multiple Masonic groups.
- **NTEE Code**: Y40 — Fraternal Beneficiary Societies and Voluntary Employees' Associations
- **Tax Status**: 501(c)(10) fraternal society; eligible to receive tax-deductible contributions.

### Mission and Activities
The association provides a physical and organizational home for Masonic lodges and affiliated groups. It facilitates regular meetings, degree work, and instruction in the moral, philosophical, and symbolic teachings of Freemasonry.

It hosts numerous gatherings throughout the year that benefit hundreds of members from various Masonic entities, as well as community, public, and private events. Its core role is maintaining and operating the Masonic Temple as a gathering place that upholds the principles of Freemasonry — including brotherhood, charity, truth, and moral uprightness.

### Financial Overview (Recent Data)
- It operates primarily through investment income, contributions, and modest rental income from the facility.
- It has no full-time employees and functions through volunteer Masonic members and officers.

### Connection to Utah Freemasonry
The temple is home to several **Blue Lodges**, York Rite bodies, Scottish Rite activities, Job’s Daughters, DeMolay, and other appendant/concordant bodies. It has a long history in Ogden, with earlier temples dating back to the early 20th century. The current facility continues to serve as a key center for Freemasonry in the Ogden area under the jurisdiction of the Grand Lodge of Utah (F&AM).

This organization embodies the traditional Masonic emphasis on fraternal support, community service, and the preservation of historic Masonic spaces. It is independent but closely aligned with the broader Utah Masonic community.
